Our Story

CodeForge Academy started as a simple idea: programming shouldn't be intimidating. Too many great people give up on coding before they ever get a chance to love it.

We built a curriculum that meets beginners where they are — step by step, project by project — so that every student finishes with real skills and real confidence.

Since 2023, we've helped hundreds of students go from "I don't know where to start" to building their first websites and landing their first tech opportunities.
